Privacy Policy

Last updated: . This is a starter template — review with legal counsel before publishing.

Who we are

ClassMate ("we", "us") provides a school companion app for students, teachers, parents and administrators. For any privacy question, contact support@classmateapp.org.

Information we collect

  • Account details provided by your school (name, role, class/cohort, email or username).
  • Content you create in the app (messages, assignments, grades, uploaded solutions and files).
  • Technical data needed to run the service (device type, app version, basic logs, push tokens).

How we use it

To provide schedules, classrooms, messaging, grades, the NOVA tutor and related features; to keep the service secure; and to support your school. We do not sell personal data.

AI features

Prompts you send to the NOVA tutor are processed by our AI provider to generate responses. Do not share sensitive personal information in tutor chats.

Data sharing

Data is shared within your school as the feature requires (e.g., a teacher sees their students' grades). We use trusted service providers (hosting, push, AI) bound by confidentiality. We disclose data if required by law.

Retention & your rights

We keep data for as long as your school account is active. You may request access, correction or deletion via your school administrator or the contact above.

Children

ClassMate is used in schools. We process student data on behalf of and under the direction of the school.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted here.
