Topic guess: derivatives

📌 Explanation:
- Idea: A derivative is the slope of the tangent line (rate of change) at a point.
- Steps:
  1) Write the function clearly and choose the rule (power rule / sum rule).
  2) Differentiate term-by-term (e.g., d/dx(x^n)=n·x^(n−1)).
  3) Simplify and (if asked) plug in the x value to get the slope at that point.
- Example: If f(x)=x^2, then f'(x)=2x, so at x=3 the slope is 6.
